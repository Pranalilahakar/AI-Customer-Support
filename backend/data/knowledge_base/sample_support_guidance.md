# Sample Support Knowledge Base

Document type: Demonstration guidance
Status: Example content — not an official company policy

## Account access

For account access problems, ask the customer to confirm the email address
associated with their account. Recommend using the official password-reset
flow. Never ask customers to disclose their password or authentication codes.

If the reset process fails, record the error message and escalate to the
authorized support team.

## Billing and duplicate charges

For a possible duplicate charge, ask the customer for the transaction date,
amount, currency, and relevant order or invoice reference. Do not request
complete payment-card numbers or security codes.

Explain that the support team needs to review the transaction before
confirming whether a refund is appropriate. Do not promise a refund or
specific processing time without verified policy information.

## Technical problems

For an application error, ask which page or feature was affected, when the
problem occurred, and whether an error message appeared.

Request reproducible steps and relevant non-sensitive diagnostic details.
Do not ask customers to share passwords, access tokens, or other secrets.

Escalate persistent or widespread failures to the responsible technical team.

## General inquiries

For product or service questions, answer only when the relevant information
is available in an approved knowledge source. If information is missing,
explain that it needs confirmation from the support team rather than guessing.
